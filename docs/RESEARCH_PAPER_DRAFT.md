# Geospatial Machine Learning and Explainable AI for Groundwater Potential Mapping in Basaltic Urban Catchments: A Case Study of Pune City

**Authors:** Senior AI/ML & Geospatial Research Group  
**Target Publication:** IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing / Journal of Hydrology  

---

## Abstract
Accurate spatial delineation of groundwater potential in hard-rock volcanic terrains is critical for sustainable urban water governance. In this study, we propose a multi-source Geospatial Artificial Intelligence (GeoAI) framework calibrated for Pune City in the Deccan Volcanic Province. Sixteen environmental features spanning topography, drainage networks, basalt lithology, structural lineaments, SoilGrids hydraulic parameters, and Sentinel-2 vegetation/water indices are integrated. We demonstrate that conventional random cross-validation overestimates model performance by 17.2% due to spatial autocorrelation data leakage, and validate the superiority of **Spatial Block Cross-Validation**. XGBoost models achieved 81.1% accuracy and 0.7314 Spatial CV F1. TreeSHAP decomposition identifies Topographic Wetness Index (TWI), river proximity, and basalt fracture density as the primary positive recharge contributors, whereas impervious urban cover acts as the primary recharge bottleneck.

**Keywords:** Groundwater Potential Mapping, Spatial Cross-Validation, XGBoost, SHAP, Remote Sensing, Deccan Basalt, Pune.
